Research Outputs

Safe RLHF-V: Safe Reinforcement Learning from Multi-modal Human Feedback
NeurIPS 2025

Authors: Jiaming Ji, Xinyu Chen, Rui Pan, Conghui Zhang, Han Zhu, Jiahao Li, Donghai Hong, Boyuan Chen, Jiayi Zhou, Kaile Wang, Juntao Dai, Chi-Min Chan, Yida Tang, Sirui Han, Yike Guo, Yaodong Yang.

Summary: Safe RLHF-V studies safety alignment for multimodal large language models, addressing harmful responses in image-text scenarios and the limitation that traditional single-preference RLHF struggles to balance helpfulness and safety. The paper introduces BeaverTails-V, Beaver-Guard-V, and a Safe RLHF-V algorithm based on decoupled reward/cost modeling and Lagrangian constrained optimization.

Personal Contribution: As co-first author, responsible for most code implementation, dataset construction, and evaluation.

CryoGEN-II: Cryogenic Electron Tomography Reconstruction via Generative Network
CVPR 2026 Workshop

Authors: Yunfei Teng, Xinyu Chen, He Zhang, Qiwei Ye, Yulei Hung, Sha Hu.

Summary: CryoGEN-II is a generative method for Cryo-ET 3D reconstruction, aiming to mitigate the missing-wedge problem caused by limited-angle acquisition and the resulting Z-axis elongation, artifacts, and structural distortion. The paper reinterprets Cryo-ET reconstruction from a variational-inference perspective and introduces latent-space Sharpness-Aware Minimization to improve training stability and reconstruction robustness.

Personal Contribution: As co-first author, responsible for in-situ Cryo-ET algorithm implementation and experiments.

CryoWGEN: Cryogenic Electron Tomography Reconstruction via Wasserstein Generative Network
In submission

Authors: Xinyu Chen, Zhichao Chen, Hongwang Xiao, Xi Chen, Yuxuan Xiong, Yan Wang, Zhouchen Lin, Yunfei Teng.

Summary: CryoWGEN proposes a new generative reconstruction framework that reformulates missing-wedge completion as variational inference and further introduces optimal transport, enabling the model to learn posterior distributions that recover isotropic resolution while preserving structural fidelity and interpretability.

Personal Contribution: As first author, responsible for all algorithm implementation, in-situ Cryo-ET experiments, and part of the paper writing.

EVIA: Entropic Variational Inference Auto-encoding
UAI 2026

Authors: Yunfei Teng, Zhichao Chen, Xinyu Chen, Lulu Tang, Sixin Zhang, Zhouchen Lin.

Summary: EVIA is an auto-encoding generative framework combining entropy regularization, optimal transport, and variational inference. It extends the deterministic latent mapping in WAE to distribution-level posterior modeling and uses SGLD sampling to improve training stability and latent-space alignment, outperforming VAE/AAE/WAE on posterior estimation, image generation, and image inpainting.

Personal Contribution: Responsible for improving experimental results and writing the experimental section.

Enhancing LLM-Based Social Bot via an Adversarial Learning Framework
EMNLP 2025 Oral

Authors: Fanqi Kong, Xiaoyuan Zhang, Xinyu Chen, Yaodong Yang, Song-Chun Zhu, Xue Feng.

Summary: EvoBot is an LLM-based social media agent framework designed to generate human-like content grounded in real user profiles and social relationships. The paper proposes an adversarial learning paradigm that first uses real social media data for supervised fine-tuning, then introduces a co-evolving social bot detector as dynamic feedback and optimizes EvoBot through DPO.

Personal Contribution: Responsible for part of the experiments, paper writing, and figure optimization.

SIV-Bench: A Video Benchmark for Social Interaction Understanding and Reasoning
ACL 2026

Authors: Fanqi Kong, Weiqin Zu, Xinyu Chen, Yaodong Yang, Song-Chun Zhu, Xue Feng.

Summary: SIV-Bench is a video benchmark for evaluating multimodal large language models on social interaction understanding and reasoning. Built on social relationship theory, it evaluates social scene understanding, social state reasoning, and social dynamics prediction across tasks such as action recognition, expression recognition, relationship reasoning, intention reasoning, factual prediction, and counterfactual prediction.

Personal Contribution: Participated in data collection and evaluation.

Reinforced Self-play for Long-Context Understanding
In preparation

Authors: Xinyu Chen, Yiding Wang, Shijia Kang, Muhan Zhang.

Summary: Long-context Zero Reasoner is a self-play reinforcement learning framework for improving long-context language models. Without human-labeled data, it automatically generates and solves Needle-in-a-Haystack long-context tasks to build a self-verifiable training loop, improving information localization and reasoning at million-token context lengths and alleviating the lost-in-the-middle problem.

Personal Contribution: Responsible for experimental code implementation, evaluation, and paper writing.

GraphVerify: A Dependency-Aware Multi-Agent Verifier for Natural Language Proofs
In preparation

Authors: Haotong Yang, Xinyu Chen, Huijun Yu, Wenkai Yu, Songshen He, Siqi Yang, Zhouchen Lin, Muhan Zhang.

Summary: GraphVerify is a multi-agent framework for verifying mathematical natural-language proofs. It parses proofs into Proof Dependency Graphs and dispatches specialized agents to verify each reasoning step, while integrating external tools to improve reliability and produce interpretable diagnostic reports.

Transfer learning discovery of molecular modulators for perovskite solar cells
